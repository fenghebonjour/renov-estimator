Quick Reference — Thread States

RUNNABLE

Executing on CPU or ready to run. Continuously RUNNABLE with no I/O lines = hot loop.

BLOCKED

Waiting for a monitor held by another thread. Always paired with a "waiting to lock" line.

WAITING

Parked until notified (Object.wait, LockSupport.park). Normal for idle threads in a pool.

TIMED_WAITING

Like WAITING but with a timeout. sleep(), join(ms), park(ns) all produce this state.
